# Taxonomy Guide: Reddit University Post Topics

This taxonomy was developed to classify the main topics discussed by students on the r/mcgill and r/conrdia subreddits. Th egoal is to compare the types of discussions taking place in the two univerisity communities. The taxonomy consists of 8 mutually exclusive categories used to classify posts from the r/mcgill and r/concordia subreddits. It was developed through the initial review of 100 sampled Reddit posts (25 from each subbreddit's New and Top listings). The /new displays the most recently submitted posts, and /top displays the highest-scoring posts over a selected period (in this case year).

-- 
## 1. Academic Assesments & Coursework (AAC) 
- **Definition**: Posts discussing academic assessments, coursework, grades, and students' experiences completing their courses.


- **Inclusions**: Exams, assignments, grading of a teacher, study strategies, group projects, academic workload, course-related difficulties.
- **Exclusions**: Course selction, registration, degree requirements, academic programs, getting into course (ex. thanks to stranger, I was able to get into PSYC215) and schedule planning (APP).

- **What's in (examples)**: 
* Questions about exams, assignments, or grading.
* Discussions about study strategies, academic workload, or difficulties with coursework.
* Students sharing their experiences with a particular course or professor's teaching and grading.

- **Edge cases**:
* A post asking whether a course is difficult to complete belongs to AAC, whereas a post asking whether to register for that course belongs to Academic Planning & Programs (APP).
* Posts about academic stress caused by exams or assignments belong to AAC, unless the main focus is a student's mental health or well-being (SWB).

## 2. Academic Planning & Programs (APP)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing academci programs, course selection, degree requirements, and decisions related to students' educational pathways.

- **Inclusions**: Course registration, schedule building, degree planning, program selection, program trasnfers, internships, planning returnal to university  and academic planning tools.
- **Exclusions**: Exams, assignments, grading, study strategies, experiences completing coursework.

- **What's in (examples)**: 
* Posts asking for advice about which courses to take or which academic program to choose.
* Questions about course registration, degree requirements, or transferring programs.

- **Edge cases**:
* Posts asking whether a course is worth taking belong to APP, even if they mention its difficulty. However, posts primarily discussing difficulties with coursework belong to AAC.
* Posts discussing internships as part of academic or career planning belong to APP, whereas posts focused on extracurricular student activities belong to SLA.

## 3. Campus Facilities & Services (CFS)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing university facilities, services, resources, and campus operations.

- **Inclusions**: Library/study spaces, campus buildings, university services, facility conditions, and disruptions to campus operations (ex. school closing).
- **Exclusions**: Complaints about student behaviour in shared spaces, housing or public transportation, and course planning tools.

- **What's in (examples)**: 
* Questions about library opening hours or available study spaces.
* Complaints about the condition or maintenance of university buildings.
* Announcements about campus closures or disruptions to university services.

- **Edge cases**:
* A post complaining about a noisy library because students are being disruptive belongs to CESB, whereas a complaint about broken equipment or inadequate facilities in the library belongs to CFS.
* Posts about course planning tools belong to APP, even if the tools are provided by the university.

## 4. Housing & Trasnportation (HT)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing housing, commuting, transportation, and related infrastructure affecting students.

- **Inclusions**: Apartment hunting, rental issues, public transportation, metro and REM stations, commuting, and construction affecting transportation.
- **Exclusions**: University buildings and campus facilities unrelated to housing or transportation.

- **What's in (examples)**: 
* Students asking for advice about finding apartments near campus.
* Discussions about metro or REM disruptions affecting students' commutes.
* Complaints about landlords, rent, or housing conditions.

- **Edge cases**:
* A post about construction affecting access to a metro station belongs to HT, whereas a post about construction disrupting a campus building belongs to CFS.
* Posts discussing dangerous situations on public transportation belong to SWB if the main focus is personal safety rather than commuting.

## 5. Campus Etiquette & Student Behavior (CESB)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing the behaviour, manners, and conduct of students or other individuals on campus.

- **Inclusions**: Disruptive behaviour in study spaces, inappropriate public behaviour, misuse of shared spaces, and complaints about campus etiquette.
- **Exclusions**: Problems with campus facilities rather than people's behaviour (CFS), and incidents primarily concerning personal safety (ex. harassement or being flashed).

- **What's in (examples)**: 
* Complaints about students talking loudly in quiet study areas.
* Posts criticizing students for leaving shared spaces dirty.
* Discussions about inappropriate or inconsiderate behaviour on campus.

- **Edge cases**:
* A complaint about students making excessive noise in a library belongs to CESB, whereas a complaint about inadequate soundproofing belongs to CFS.
* Posts about harassment, threats, or other behaviour that puts someone's safety at risk belong to SWB rather than CESB.


## 6. Politics and Social Issues (PSI)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing political events, government policies, laws, and broader social issues affecting students or the university community.

- **Inclusions**: Political developments, legislation, discrimination, social justice issues(ex. letting a murderer attend university), and government decisions affecting students.
- **Exclusions**: University administrative decisions unrelated to broader political or social issues.

- **What's in (examples)**: 
* Discussions about government policies affecting university students.
* Posts about discrimination or social justice issues within the university community.
* Discussions about political events or legislation relevant to students.

- **Edge cases**:
* A post about a government policy affecting university tuition belongs to PSI, whereas a post asking how to pay tuition or manage course registration belongs to APP.
* Posts about discrimination belong to PSI when discussing broader social issues, but personal experiences primarily involving threats or safety concerns belong to SWB.

## 7. Safety & Well-being (SWB)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing personal safety, physical or mental health, and students' well-being.

- **Inclusions**: Dangerous situations, threatening incidents, accidents, mental health concerns, and experiences affecting personal safety or well-being.
- **Exclusions**: General complaints about student behaviour that do not involve safety concerns, and routine academic stress or workload complaints.

- **What's in (examples)**: 
* Posts warning students about dangerous situations near campus.
* Students discussing mental health concerns or seeking support.
* Reports of harassment, threatening incidents, or accidents affecting students.

- **Edge cases**:
* A post complaining about a heavy academic workload belongs to AAC, whereas a post primarily discussing its effects on a student's mental health belongs to SWB.
* Complaints about inconsiderate student behaviour belong to CESB unless the behaviour involves threats, harassment, or personal safety concerns.

## 8. Student Life & Activities (SLA)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing students' social experiences, extracurricular activities, and participation in the university community.

- **Inclusions**: Student clubs, campus events, social gatherings, friendships, student communities, and extracurricular opportunities.
- **Exclusions**: Complaints about inappropriate student behaviour, academic coursework, and university facilities or services.

- **What's in (examples)**:
* Students looking for friends or people with shared interests.
* Announcements about student club events or social gatherings.
* Questions about joining student organizations or participating in extracurricular activities.
 
- **Edge cases**:
* A post about joining a student club belongs to SLA, whereas a post about joining an academic program or registering for courses belongs to APP.
* Posts discussing group projects or other course requirements belong to AAC, even if they involve interacting with other students.
