# Coding Codebook: Reddit Subreddit Post Typology

This codebok defines 8 mutually exclusive categories used to classify posts from the r/mcgill and r/concordia subreddits. It was developed through the initial review of 100 sampled Reddit posts (25 from each subbreddit's New and Top listings).

---

## 1. Academic Assesments & Coursework (AAC) 
- **Definition**: Posts discussing academic assessments, coursework, grades, and students' experiences completing their courses.
- **Inclusions**: Exams, assignments, grading of a teacher, study strategies, group projects, academic workload, course-related difficulties.
- **Exclusions**: Course selction, registration, degree requirements, academic programs, getting into course (ex. thanks to stranger, I was able to get into PSYC215) and schedule planning (APP).

## 2. Academic Planning & Programs (APP)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing academci programs, course selection, degree requirements, and decisions related to students' educational pathways.
- **Inclusions**: Course registration, schedule building, degree planning, program selection, program trasnfers, internships, planning returnal to university  and academic planning tools.
- **Exclusions**: Exams, assignments, grading, study strategies, experiences completing coursework.

## 3. Campus Facilities & Services (CFS)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing university facilities, services, resources, and campus operations.
- **Inclusions**: Library/study spaces, campus buildings, university services, facility conditions, and disruptions to campus operations (ex. school closing).
- **Exclusions**: Complaints about student behaviour in shared spaces, housing or public transportation, and course planning tools.

## 4. Housing & Trasnportation (HT)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing housing, commuting, transportation, and related infrastructure affecting students.
- **Inclusions**: Apartment hunting, rental issues, public transportation, metro and REM stations, commuting, and construction affecting transportation.
- **Exclusions**: University buildings and campus facilities unrelated to housing or transportation.

## 5. Campus Etiquette & Student Behavior (CESB)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing the behaviour, manners, and conduct of students or other individuals on campus.
- **Inclusions**: Disruptive behaviour in study spaces, inappropriate public behaviour, misuse of shared spaces, and complaints about campus etiquette.
- **Exclusions**: Problems with campus facilities rather than people's behaviour (CFS), and incidents primarily concerning personal safety (ex. harassement or being flashed).

## 6. Politics and Social Issues (PSI)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing political events, government policies, laws, and broader social issues affecting students or the university community.
- **Inclusions**: Political developments, legislation, discrimination, social justice issues(ex. letting a murderer attend university), and government decisions affecting students.
- **Exclusions**: University administrative decisions unrelated to broader political or social issues.

## 7. Safety & Well-being (SWB)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing personal safety, physical or mental health, and students' well-being.
- **Inclusions**: Dangerous situations, threatening incidents, accidents, mental health concerns, and experiences affecting personal safety or well-being.
- **Exclusions**: General complaints about student behaviour that do not involve safety concerns, and routine academic stress or workload complaints.


## 8. Student Life & Activities (SLA)
- **Definition**: Posts discussing students' social experiences, extracurricular activities, and participation in the university community.
- **Inclusions**: Student clubs, campus events, social gatherings, friendships, student communities, and extracurricular opportunities.
- **Exclusions**: Complaints about inappropriate student behaviour, academic coursework, and university facilities or services.
